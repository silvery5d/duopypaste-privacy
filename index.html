Duopy&Paste — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-29

Duopy&Paste does not collect, transmit, store, or share any data.

The app reads the macOS clipboard only to clean text locally on your device when you trigger it (by copying the same text twice in quick succession), and writes the cleaned result back to your clipboard. Recently cleaned items are kept only in memory and are discarded when the app quits.

Duopy&Paste has no network access. No clipboard content or any other data ever leaves your Mac.

Duopy&Paste — 隐私政策

最后更新：2026-05-29

Duopy&Paste 不收集、不传输、不存储、不分享任何数据。

仅在你触发时（快速连续复制同一段文本两次），本应用才读取 macOS 剪贴板、 在你的设备上就地清洗文本，并把清洗结果写回剪贴板。最近清洗的条目仅保存在内存中， 应用退出即清除。

Duopy&Paste 没有任何网络访问。没有任何剪贴板内容 或其它数据会离开你的 Mac。
