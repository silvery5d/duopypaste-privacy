Duopy&Paste — Support

Last updated: 2026-05-30

Duopy&Paste is a macOS menu-bar utility that cleans formatting artifacts (hard line breaks, indentation, quote bars, table borders, inline Markdown markers) from text you copy out of terminals and AI coding assistants.

How to use

  1. Select the text in any app.
  2. Press ⌘C twice within 1.5 seconds (a "double-copy").
  3. The cleaned text is now on your clipboard — press ⌘V to paste it anywhere.

A single ⌘C never triggers cleaning. Duopy&Paste only acts when you deliberately double-press, so there's no false capture.

Common questions

Nothing happened when I double-copied

  • The two presses must be within 1.5 seconds.
  • The cleaner only writes back if the content actually changed. If your selection had no formatting artifacts to clean, the clipboard stays the same — that's expected.
  • Check that the menu-bar icon is present (the app is running).

How do I see the cleaning history?

Click the menu-bar icon. The dropdown panel shows the last 10 cleaned items side by side — "original" on the left, "cleaned" on the right. Click either column to copy that version back to your clipboard.

Does the history persist across restarts?

No. The history is kept only in memory and is discarded when you quit the app.

Does it work with non-ASCII text (Chinese, Japanese, etc.)?

Yes. The cleaner is CJK-aware — it joins wrapped CJK lines without inserting a space (which would be ugly), while joining wrapped English lines with one space so they read naturally.

Will it break my code blocks or tables?

No. Cleaning is conservative: code blocks, lists, and table structure are preserved. Only the noise around them is removed.

How do I enable launch-at-login?

Click the menu-bar icon to open the panel and toggle Launch at Login.

Privacy

Duopy&Paste has no network access. Nothing is collected, transmitted, stored, or shared. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Contact

Bug reports, feature requests, or general questions: github.com/silvery5d

Duopy&Paste — 技术支持

最后更新：2026-05-30

Duopy&Paste 是一个 macOS 菜单栏小工具，专为清洗从终端和 AI 编程助手复制出来的「格式痕迹」（硬换行、缩进、引用竖线、表格边框、行内 Markdown 标记）。

使用方法

  1. 在任意 app 里选中需要清洗的文本。
  2. 1.5 秒内按两次 ⌘C（连按两次复制）。
  3. 剪贴板里已是清洗后的版本——按 ⌘V 即可粘到任何地方。

单次 ⌘C 永远不会触发清洗，只有你「连按两次」时才动作， 没有误抓。

常见问题

我双击复制了，但好像什么都没发生

  • 两次按键必须在 1.5 秒内。
  • 清洗器只在内容真的有变化时才写回剪贴板。如果你复制的本来就是干净文本（没有格式痕迹可清），剪贴板保持原样是正常的。
  • 确认菜单栏图标存在（app 正在运行）。

怎么看清洗历史？

点击菜单栏图标。下拉面板里最近 10 条清洗记录并排显示——左侧是 「原文」，右侧是「清洗后」。点击其中任一栏即可重新复制回剪贴板。

历史会持久保存吗？

不会。历史仅保存在内存中，应用退出即清除。

支持中文/日文/韩文等非 ASCII 文本吗？

支持。清洗器对 CJK 智能识别——中文折行合并时不会插空格（避免变丑）， 英文折行合并时则会保留一个空格，读起来都自然。

会不会破坏我的代码块或表格？

不会。清洗策略是保守的：代码块、列表、表格的内部结构完整保留， 只去掉它们周围的杂质。

怎么开启开机自启？

点开菜单栏面板，打开登录时启动开关。

隐私

Duopy&Paste 没有任何网络访问，不收集、不传输、不存储、 不分享任何数据。详见隐私政策。
